    Common questions

    Before you book

    Is the base price the final price?

    For the scope listed, yes. Anything outside scope — extra pages, bespoke integrations, large content migrations — is quoted in writing before work begins. We do not issue surprise invoices.

    Which payment methods do you accept?

    MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, and M-Pesa to +256 744 498583. International cards via invoice for GBP and ZAR clients. Direct bank transfers are unavailable at the moment.

    Do you work with clients outside East Africa?

    Regularly. The studio runs on East Africa Time with overlap hours for European and Gulf time zones; async updates land in email or WhatsApp daily either way.

    What happens after launch?

    Every package includes aftercare — a defined window of free fixes and questions. Beyond that, optional care plans cover updates, backups, and small content changes monthly.

    How current are the exchange rates?

    The site fetches live rates on load from a public forex endpoint and caches the last good result. If you are offline, cached or reference rates are used and labeled as such in the header notes.
